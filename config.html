Index Ring

Connect the watch app to your Index account.

Paste your token

Paste any of these and tap Save:

  • a refreshToken (starts with AMf-)
  • a custom token (a long eyJ… value from Rebble)
  • your whole credentials.json, or a {"customToken":"…"} block

How to get a token on your phone

  1. Open index.rebble.com in your normal browser (not this screen) and sign in with Google.
  2. Go to index.rebble.com/api/auth/firebase/custom-token — it shows JSON. Copy the value between the quotes after "customToken":.
  3. Come back here and paste it above, then tap Save.

Or, if you have a refreshToken already (e.g. from ~/.config/pebble-pebble-index/credentials.json on your computer), just paste that.

Get it automatically (desktop only)

This screen runs in its own little browser inside the Pebble app, so it can't see your index.rebble.com sign-in. It only works if you're signed in in this same screen, which phones normally can't do.

Privacy: your token is only sent to Google's sign-in endpoint and the Nenya API. It is stored on your phone, not on this page.
