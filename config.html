Index Ring

Connect the watch app to your Index account by pasting a token below.

Paste your token

Accepts a refreshToken (starts with AMf-), a customToken (a long eyJ…), or your whole credentials.json.

Where to get a token

Easiest — from your computer. You already have a refreshToken in ~/.config/pebble-pebble-index/credentials.json (or run node src/cli/auth.mjs). Copy the refreshToken value, get it onto your phone (email, notes, etc.), and paste it above.

On the phone. This screen runs inside the Pebble app and can't see your browser sign-in, so do this in your normal browser:

  1. Open index.rebble.com and sign in with Google.
  2. Then open index.rebble.com/api/auth/firebase/custom-token. It shows JSON: copy the text after "customToken": (inside the quotes).
  3. Paste that here and tap Save.

If step 2 shows a blank page or downloads a file, you're probably not signed in yet — do step 1 first, in the same browser. Some phones need you to open the link, then use "Request desktop site" once.

Privacy: your token is only sent to Google's sign-in endpoint and the Nenya API. It is stored on your phone, not on this page.
