Connect the watch app to your Index account by pasting a token below.

Paste your token

Accepts a refreshToken (starts with AMf-), a customToken (a long eyJ…), or the JSON they come in.

How to get a token

Most reliable — on a computer.

  1. Sign in at index.rebble.com.
  2. Open the browser's DevTools console (F12 → Console) and run this line. Copy what it prints (it will look like {"customToken":"eyJ…"}): 
    fetch('/api/auth/firebase/custom-token',{credentials:'include'}).then(r=>r.text()).then(t=>console.log(t))
  3. Paste the printed value into the box above and tap Save.

Opening index.rebble.com/api/auth/firebase/custom-token directly in a tab does not work — the endpoint only answers a signed-in request made from the index.rebble.com page itself, which is exactly what the console line above does.

Already set up on your computer? If you've run node src/cli/auth.mjs, your refreshToken is in ~/.config/pebble-pebble-index/credentials.json — just copy that refreshToken value to your phone and paste it above.

Get it onto the phone however is easy (email it to yourself, a notes app, etc.).

Privacy: your token is only sent to Google's sign-in endpoint and the Nenya API. It is stored on your phone, not on this page.
