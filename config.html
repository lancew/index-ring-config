Index Ring

Paste your Nenya refreshToken below. It is stored on this phone and used only to fetch short-lived sign-in tokens from Google's token endpoint. It is not sent anywhere else.

A token is already configured on this phone. Pasting a new one will replace it.

How to get one:

  1. Sign in at index.repebble.com in your browser.
  2. In the project terminal run node src/cli/auth.mjs, or open ~/.config/pebble-pebble-index/credentials.json and copy the refreshToken value.
  3. Paste it above and press Save.
