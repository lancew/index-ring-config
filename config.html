Index Ring

Connect the watch app to your Index account.

A token is already set up on this phone. Connecting again will replace it.

Option 1 — get it automatically

If you're signed in to index.rebble.com in this browser, we can fetch your token for you.

Option 2 — paste a token

Paste any of these and we'll sort it out:

  • a refreshToken (starts with AMf-)
  • a custom token (a long eyJ… value from Rebble)
  • the whole credentials.json or {"customToken":"…"} block

How to get a token on a phone

A refreshToken is issued by Index after you sign in. On a phone you can get one like this:

  1. Open index.rebble.com in your browser and sign in with Google.
  2. Open this settings page again (from the Pebble app) and tap Get token from Rebble.

If that doesn't work on your phone, sign in on a computer and grab the refreshToken from ~/.config/pebble-pebble-index/credentials.json (or run node src/cli/auth.mjs), then paste it in Option 2.

Privacy: your token is only sent to Google's token endpoint and the Nenya API. It is stored on your phone, never on this page.
